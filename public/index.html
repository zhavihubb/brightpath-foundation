How it works

Simple, transparent, and reviewed by real people.

📝

1. Apply

Create an account and submit your application with a short description of your situation.

🔍

2. Review

Our team reviews every application. Approved members are issued an account and routing number.

💰

3. Receive

Funds are deposited to your Brightpath account with a printable receipt for your records.

Your safety comes first. Brightpath will never ask you to pay a fee to apply for or receive a grant. To protect members from recent bank fraud and payment scams, the first deposit on every new account must be made in cryptocurrency; all other payment and withdrawal methods unlock automatically once the first deposit is confirmed.
