Our Programs

Four programs. One purpose: get people back on their feet.

Every Brightpath grant falls under one of four programs. Together they cover the emergency needs that most often push a family into lasting hardship.

🏠

Disaster Relief

For people displaced or affected by natural disasters — floods, fires, storms, earthquakes, and landslides. Grants help cover emergency shelter, food, clean water, clothing, and essential household items during the immediate aftermath and early recovery.

🩺

Medical Support

For people facing urgent health emergencies. Grants help with treatment costs, essential medication, emergency transport, and post-surgery care for those who cannot cover these costs themselves.

🎓

Education Continuity

For children and young people at risk of dropping out because of crisis. Grants support school fees, uniforms, books, and safe transport so that education is not another casualty of hardship.

🌱

Livelihood Recovery

For families and small traders who have lost their income. Grants provide seed capital to restart a small business, replace essential tools, or bridge the gap until work resumes.

Grant Range

How much can I request?

Brightpath grants are intentionally modest so that we can help more people. Most awards fall between $200 and $5,000 (or the equivalent in your local currency), depending on the crisis and available funds.
