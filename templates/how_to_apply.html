How to Apply

Applying takes minutes. It is always free.

Follow these steps to submit a grant application. You can track the status of every application from your personal dashboard.

Create a free account

Register with your full name, email, and country. You will use this account to apply and to check your status. Registration is free and takes under a minute.

Open a new application

From your dashboard, choose "New application". Select the program that fits your situation — disaster relief, medical support, education continuity, or livelihood recovery.

Describe your situation

Tell us, in your own words, what happened and what you need. Enter the amount you are requesting and your household size. Be honest and specific — it helps us help you.

Submit and track

Once submitted, your application status begins as "Submitted". Our review team then moves it to "Under Review" and finally to "Approved" or "Declined". You will see every change in your dashboard.

Receive your grant

Approved applicants are notified in the dashboard with the awarded amount. Funds are disbursed through a verified channel. You will never be asked to pay a fee to receive your grant.

What you'll need

  • ✓ A valid email address you can access.
  • ✓ Your country of residence.
  • ✓ A short description of your situation.
  • ✓ The amount you are requesting and why.

⚠️ A word about safety

Brightpath will never ask you to pay a fee, send money, or share bank passwords to receive a grant. If anyone claiming to be from Brightpath asks for payment, it is a scam. Report it through our contact page immediately.
