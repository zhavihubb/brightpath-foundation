How withdrawals work

  • ✓ Choose any enabled method — crypto (BTC, ETH, USDT), bank transfer, or online wallet.
  • ✓ Copy the wallet or account details shown above and use them as your destination.
  • ✓ Your requested amount is held from your balance while the request is pending.
  • ✓ An administrator reviews every request and approves or declines it from the admin dashboard.
  • ✓ If a request is declined, the held funds are returned to your available balance and you are told why.

Brightpath will never ask you to pay a fee to withdraw your funds. If anyone requests payment, please report it via support immediately.
