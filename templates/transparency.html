How the funds are used

Brightpath is a grant-giving foundation. Funds are awarded directly to individuals and families in crisis and disbursed into their Brightpath member accounts. Our operating model keeps overhead low so that the majority of every grant reaches a person in need.

🔒

Our anti-fraud promise

Brightpath never charges applicants a fee and never asks for payment details to release funds. Every disbursement is recorded with a receipt, and members are notified of every activity on their account. Any message claiming otherwise is a scam and should be reported to us.
