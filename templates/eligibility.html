Eligibility

Who can apply for a Bright Future Grant grant?

We keep our criteria simple and fair so that people in genuine need can access help quickly.

✅

You are likely eligible if you:

  • ✓ Are 18 years or older.
  • ✓ Are experiencing a genuine crisis — disaster, medical emergency, loss of income, or interruption to a child's education.
  • ✓ Live in any country in the world. There is no geographic restriction.
  • ✓ Can describe your situation clearly and truthfully.
  • ✓ Have not already received a Bright Future Grant grant for the same event.
⛔

You are not eligible if:

  • ✕ The request is for a commercial investment, gambling, or a for-profit venture.
  • ✕ The information provided is false or misleading.
  • ✕ You are applying on behalf of a business rather than a person or household.

How applications are reviewed

Every application is reviewed by our team against three questions: Is the need genuine? Is it urgent? Can our available funds meet it? Applications are assessed in the order received, with priority given to life-threatening and disaster-related situations.
