FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we hear most from applicants, members, and partners.

Is it really free to apply?

Yes. Applying for and receiving a Bright Future Grant grant is completely free. We will never ask you to pay a fee, send money, or share bank passwords to receive help.

How much can I receive?

Most grants range from about $200 to $5,000, with an average of around $2,000, depending on your situation and available funds. You may request any amount, and our team will assess it.

Do I need to live in a particular country?

No. Bright Future Grant accepts applications from every country. All applications are reviewed against the same published criteria.

How long does a decision take?

Most applications receive an initial review within one to three business days. Life-threatening and disaster-related situations are prioritised.

How will I know if I'm approved?

You will see your application status change in your dashboard — from "Submitted" to "Under Review" to "Approved" or "Declined". If approved, the awarded amount appears on your application.

Can I apply more than once?

You may submit more than one application for different crises, but you cannot receive two grants for the same event. Please be honest — false information leads to automatic decline.

How do I receive my grant?

Once your application is approved, Bright Future Grant issues you a member account number and routing number. Grant funds are deposited directly into that account. You'll get an email and a dashboard notification for every deposit, along with a clickable receipt you can view, print, or save as a PDF.

How will I be notified about activity on my account?

Every activity on your account — a deposit, an application decision, or a support reply — generates both a dashboard notification and an email. Nothing happens on your account without you being told.

Can I contact support?

Yes. Log in and open the Support chat from your dashboard to message our team about any issue or question. You can attach images and documents, and we'll reply right there — you'll also get an email notification.

Someone asked me to pay to receive a Bright Future Grant grant. Is that real?

No — that is a scam. Bright Future Grant never requests payment from applicants. Please report any such contact to us through the contact page.
